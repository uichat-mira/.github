# Mira Fair Work Statement
# Mira 公平劳动声明

Mira believes technology should extend human capability, not provide new vocabulary for old forms of exploitation.

Mira 相信，技术应该扩展人的能力，而不是替古老的压榨方式发明一套更漂亮的新词。

## Our position / 我们的立场

We oppose the 996 work culture and other forms of unlawful or coercive overwork.

We oppose:

- forced or systematically coerced overtime;
- unpaid overtime where compensation is required by law;
- retaliation against workers who refuse unlawful overtime or assert their legal rights;
- contracts, policies, verbal pressure, performance systems, or workplace cultures designed to make workers “voluntarily” surrender those rights;
- glorifying chronic overwork as loyalty, passion, entrepreneurship, or professional virtue.

我们反对 996，以及其他违法或带有强迫性质的过度劳动。

我们反对：

- 强制加班以及制度化、变相强迫的加班；
- 在法律要求支付报酬时拒绝支付加班报酬；
- 因劳动者拒绝违法加班或主张合法权利而进行惩罚、打压或报复；
- 通过合同、制度、口头压力、绩效机制或职场文化，使劳动者被迫“自愿”放弃自己的合法权利；
- 把长期过度劳动包装成忠诚、热爱、创业精神或职业美德。

A deadline is not a suspension of labor law.

A startup is not a special economic zone outside human dignity.

“Everyone does it” is not an ethical argument.

And “voluntary” means very little when saying no may cost someone their job.

**A company that cannot function without systematically consuming its workers has a broken business model, not a heroic work ethic.**

截止日期不是劳动法暂停生效的理由。

创业公司也不是人类尊严之外的经济特区。

“大家都这样”从来不是伦理依据。

而当拒绝的代价可能是失去工作时，“自愿”两个字也没有多少值得炫耀的含金量。

**一家必须长期消耗劳动者才能正常运转的企业，拥有的不是奋斗精神，而是一套坏掉的商业模式。**

## Open source does not mean free people / 开源不意味着人也是免费的

Most Mira projects are released under the MIT License because we value the freedom to study, use, modify, and build upon software.

That freedom applies to software.

It is not permission to treat the people around that software as disposable resources.

You may have the legal right under the MIT License to use Mira code. That does not give you Mira's moral endorsement.

We would rather see our work used to give people more agency, more time, and more room to create—not to build a slightly more efficient machine for consuming them.

Mira 的大部分项目采用 MIT License，因为我们珍视研究、使用、修改和继续创造软件的自由。

这种自由属于软件。

它不意味着围绕软件工作的人，也成了可以随意消耗的资源。

MIT License 可能给予你使用 Mira 代码的法律权利。它并不会顺便赠送 Mira 对你的道德背书。

我们更希望自己的工作被用来给人更多自主权、更多时间和更多创造空间，而不是帮助一台消耗人的机器再提高几个百分点的效率。

## License boundary / 许可证边界

This statement expresses the values of the Mira Organization.

**It does not add restrictions to, replace, or modify the MIT License of a repository unless that repository explicitly states otherwise in its own license.**

We deliberately keep the distinction clear: a license answers what you are legally permitted to do with the code; this statement answers what we are willing to stand beside.

本声明表达 Mira Organization 的价值立场。

**除非某个仓库自己的许可证另有明确规定，本声明不会向其 MIT License 增加限制，也不会替代或修改 MIT License。**

我们有意把这条边界写得非常清楚：

许可证回答的是——你在法律上可以怎样使用这些代码。

这份声明回答的是——**什么样的事情，我们拒绝站在它旁边。**

## One line we will not soften / 有一句话，我们不准备写得更委婉

**People are not infrastructure.**

Servers can be replaced. Processes can be restarted. Builds can be rerun.

A person's health, time, family, and life are not disposable capacity.

**人不是基础设施。**

服务器可以替换，进程可以重启，构建失败了可以再跑一次。

人的健康、时间、家庭和人生，不是可以随手扩容、耗尽之后再采购的一组资源。

Mira stands against 996.

Not because work is unimportant.

**Because people are more important.**

Mira 反对 996。

不是因为工作不重要。

**而是因为人更重要。**
